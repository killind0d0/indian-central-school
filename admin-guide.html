Staff Delegation & The Toggleable Approval Queue

How the administrator chooses between strict review vs. instant publishing.

MODE 1: Approval Required (Default)

Queue Mode (Strict Governance)

When a teacher creates, updates, or deletes a circular or faculty profile, their submission is saved into the Pending Approvals Queue. The live website is not touched until an administrator reviews and approves it.

  • Teacher button says: "Submit for Admin Approval".
  • Admin receives notification counter badge on Approvals & Staff tab.
  • Admin inspects details and clicks Approve or Reject with feedback.
MODE 2: Direct Publish (Fast Mode)

Direct Mode (Events & Rapid Updates)

During major events, sports days, or parent-teacher meets, the administrator can toggle approval OFF. Teachers can now publish circulars directly to the live website without waiting in a queue.

  • Teacher button automatically updates to: "Save Notice Live".
  • Published immediately to data/notices.json and live website.
  • Complete Accountability: Every action is automatically logged in the Activity Audit Log with teacher name and timestamp.

How to Toggle the Approval Setting at Any Time:

  1. Log in as Administrator using passcode ics@2026.
  2. Click the "Approvals & Staff" tab in the navigation bar.
  3. Click the "Portal Security & Access" sub-tab.
  4. Locate the card titled "Require Admin Approval for Staff Updates": • ON (Checked): Staff updates require admin review in queue.
    • OFF (Unchecked): Staff updates publish directly to live site.
  5. The toggle takes effect immediately and writes to data/permissions.json.

Security Governance & Activity Audit Log

Complete institutional accountability and incident prevention.

Permanent Audit Log: Every action performed on the portal—whether an administrator approving a proposal, or a teacher publishing directly, editing a staff member, or deleting a record—is permanently tracked in the Activity Audit Log table.

Changing the Staff Passcode: In the Portal Security & Access tab, the administrator can enter a new passcode (e.g. faculty@2026) and click "Update Passcode". All teachers will immediately need to use the new passcode to log in.

Master Lockout Switch: If the administrator toggles off "Teacher Login Allowed", all teachers are locked out of the portal immediately. This is recommended during holiday closures or exams.
