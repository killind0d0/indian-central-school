# Indian Central School - Website Portal

A responsive web portal designed for **Indian Central School** (A.T. Gate, Gaya Ji, on Gaya - Sherghati Road, Bihar).

## Live Website
- **Official Domain**: [https://icsgayaji.org](https://icsgayaji.org)
- **GitHub Pages**: [https://killind0d0.github.io/indian-central-school/](https://killind0d0.github.io/indian-central-school/)

## Key Features Built
1. **Top Notice Ticker**: Live admissions notice for Session 2026–27 + direct contact details + Parent App link.
2. **Hero Section**: Academic badges (Est. 2000, 25+ Years Legacy, 4.5★ Rating) + dual Call-to-Actions.
3. **Institutional Stats Counter**: Key trust metrics for parents (25+ Years Excellence).
4. **Principal's Desk Message**: Mission, values, and leadership statement.
5. **Campus Facilities Grid**: Smart Classrooms, Science Labs, Computer Lab, Library, Transport Fleet, Sports & Cultural hall.
6. **Official Mobile App Feature**: Spotlight for the official Android application **"Indian Central School, Gaya"** on Google Play (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=indian.schoolapp.indian) for attendance, homework, and live bus GPS tracking.
7. **Official School Crest & Favicon**: High-resolution transparent PNG crest (assets/logo.png) featuring the institutional shield with Veena and open book for "Indian Central School - A.T. Gate, Gaya Ji".
8. **Dual-Channel Admission Enquiry Dispatch**:
   - Simultaneously sends full enquiry details to **indiancentralschool@gmail.com** via background AJAX.
   - Concurrently opens **WhatsApp direct chat** with pre-filled details to the private admission gateway (918877592738), keeping the phone number shielded from scrapers.
9. **Dynamic 18-Review Synchronized Carousel**:
   - Curated bank of **18 diverse, verified parent, guardian, and alumni reviews** sourced from Justdial (5.0★), Google Reviews, and Google Play Store.
   - **Auto-Sync Non-Repeating Carousel**: Smoothly cycles forward page-by-page every 5.5 seconds without duplicating reviews on-screen.
   - **Smart Pause on Hover**: Automatically halts auto-rotation when the user hovers over any card.
   - **Interactive Category Filtering**: Filter pills for All (18), Academics & Faculty (6), Transport & App (3), Discipline & Safety (5), and Early Years & Sports (4).
   - **Grid vs Carousel Toggle**: Visitors can switch between the carousel and full "Show All Grid".
10. **Interactive Google Maps Embed**: Pre-configured with coordinates (24.561910, 84.795502) on Gaya - Sherghati Road.
11. **Total Visitor Counter (HitWebCounter Style 0009)**: Discreet odometer-style unique visitor tracking widget embedded in the footer.
12. **Mobile Sticky Action Bar**: Instant "Call School", "WhatsApp", and "Apply Now" buttons fixed at the bottom for smartphone visitors.
13. **Agency Branding & Advertisement**: Interactive footer promotional banner for [**SJ Digitals**](https://sjdigitals.vercel.app) to capture prospective web design and school portal clients.
