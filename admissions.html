Session 2026–2027 Guidelines

Simple 4-Step Admission Procedure

We welcome prospective families to experience our campus culture. Seats are allotted on a first-come, first-served basis subject to class seat availability.

1

Submit Online Enquiry or Visit Campus

Fill out the quick form on this page or visit the school office at A.T. Gate to collect the official admission prospectus.

2

Informal Interaction & Baseline Evaluation

A friendly, stress-free interaction for foundational kids and baseline assessment in English & Maths for junior/secondary classes.

3

Document Verification & Registration

Submission of child's birth certificate or school transfer certificate (TC), photographs, and student ID proof.

4

Welcome to the ICS Family

Issuance of admission enrollment number, uniform & book kit allocation, and mobile app parent portal credentials.

Age Eligibility Criteria (As of March 31, 2026)

Class / Grade Minimum Age Curriculum Focus
Play Group / Nursery 3+ Years Sensory exploration & verbal discovery
LKG & UKG 4+ & 5+ Years Phonics, numeracy & handwriting
Class 1 to 5 (Primary) 6+ Years onwards English, EVS, Mathematics & Computers
Class 6 to 8 (Middle) 11+ Years onwards Physics, Chemistry, Biology, S.St. & IT
Class 9 & 10 (Secondary) 14+ Years onwards CBSE Board syllabus & mentor coaching

Documents Required at Admission

  • Municipal Birth Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from past school
  • 4 Passport-size Student Photos
  • Parent / Guardian Aadhaar Copies
  • Previous Grade Report Card
  • Caste / Income Certificate (if applicable)
