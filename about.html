About Our Institution

A Legacy of Discipline, Knowledge, and Modern Learning

Founded in 2000, Indian Central School has stood as a cornerstone of quality English medium education at A.T. Gate, Gaya Ji. Situated along the Gaya - Sherghati Road, we cater to students from foundational kindergarten up to secondary schooling, giving every student equal opportunities to flourish in academics, sports, and life skills.

Personal Attention

Balanced student-teacher ratio ensuring every child is individually mentored.

Holistic Learning

Co-curricular cultural events, debates, Olympiads, and moral values.

Safe & Secured Campus

CCTV monitored boundary premises, RO water, and attentive support staff.

Character & Discipline

Instilling strong moral science, civic responsibility, and leadership.
